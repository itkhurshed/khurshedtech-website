About

SOC-focused Cybersecurity Specialist, hardened by 15+ years in IT.

I'm a Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer, Cloud Administrator, and Cybersecurity Specialist with over 15 years of experience keeping enterprise environments online, monitored, and defensible — across financial services, energy, and technology sectors. My work spans enterprise infrastructure ownership end-to-end: cloud administration on Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365 and identity operations, Cisco-based enterprise networking, and SOC-style security operations — SIEM monitoring, log analysis, threat detection, and incident response.

I hold CompTIA CySA+, Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104), and Cisco CCNP Enterprise / CCNA credentials. I care about building infrastructure that doesn't just work — it holds up under pressure, scales cleanly, and stays defensible against real-world threats, and I'm now targeting senior IT infrastructure, cloud, and cybersecurity roles across Europe, the Gulf, and international technology companies.

  • Cloud administration — Microsoft Azure, identity & hybrid infrastructure
  • Enterprise Microsoft 365, Exchange Online, SharePoint, Teams & Intune administration
  • Cisco enterprise networking — routing, switching, firewall & VPN (CCNP/CCNA)
  • SIEM monitoring, threat detection, incident response & vulnerability management
  • Windows Server, Active Directory & Group Policy management
  • Financial-sector enterprise IT infrastructure & security operations
