Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond to support requests?

Business-hours plans are answered same business day; Business and Enterprise plans receive priority response, with 24/7 remote assistance available on request.

Do you provide remote support?

Yes, most issues are resolved remotely the same day. Remote support is included in every plan.

Do you support Microsoft 365?

Yes. Tenant setup, mailbox migration, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and Azure administration are all part of our core service.

Do you visit customer offices?

Yes, on-site visits across Kuwait are included in the Business and Enterprise plans, and available on request for the Starter plan.

Do you support small businesses?

Yes, the Starter plan is built specifically for small offices and startups, with the same certified support as our larger clients.

Do you support businesses outside Kuwait?

Yes. Remote IT support, Microsoft 365 and cloud services can be delivered anywhere in the world. On-site visits are currently offered in Kuwait.

What is included in the monthly support plans?

Each plan includes remote IT support and a defined scope of Microsoft 365, networking and security coverage. Hardware repair, replacement parts, software licences and third-party subscription fees are billed separately.

Can I cancel or change my plan at any time?

Yes, monthly plans can be upgraded, downgraded or cancelled with notice. Annual contracts include a discount versus paying monthly.

Do you sign an NDA or confidentiality agreement?

Yes, an NDA can be signed before any engagement that involves access to sensitive business systems or data.

Do you provide emergency support?

Yes. Business and Enterprise plans include priority emergency response, and 24/7 remote emergency assistance is available on request for any plan — just reach out via WhatsApp and we'll respond as quickly as possible.

Do you provide annual maintenance contracts?

Yes. Annual maintenance contracts are available for all plans and include a discount versus paying monthly, plus a single yearly billing cycle for easier budgeting.
