The Fine Print, Made Clear

Scope, Payments & Ownership

So there are no surprises for either of us, here's exactly how scope, payments, hosting and ownership work.

📋

What "Advanced Features" Include

Depending on your approved scope, this can include a booking system, quotation form, member/login area, multilingual pages, CRM integration or workflow automation. Exact scope is confirmed in writing before development starts — all custom work is subject to approved scope.

💳

Payment Terms

A 50% deposit is required to begin the project, with the remaining 50% due before final launch. Larger projects may use milestone-based payments instead.

🔑

Website Ownership

After full payment, you receive ownership of the approved website files and supplied content. Third-party themes, plugins, fonts, images, APIs and services remain subject to their original licences.

🛍️

Payment Gateways (E-Commerce)

Payment-gateway registration, transaction charges, merchant-account fees and third-party subscriptions are not included unless stated in the quotation.

🌐

Hosting & SSL

Domain prices depend on the extension you choose. Hosting prices depend on storage and traffic needs — shared hosting starts from 10 KWD/year, business hosting is quoted separately. SSL may be free with selected hosting plans. Renewal fees may change year to year.

📐

Enterprise Page Structure

The Custom/Enterprise package uses a custom page structure based on your approved project scope — not an unlimited page count — to keep timelines, quality and pricing predictable.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

Timelines depend on the package: Starter sites typically take 3–5 working days, Basic 5–8 days, Standard 7–14 days, E-Commerce 14–25 days, and Business Pro 15–30 days. Enterprise timelines are based on approved scope. All timelines begin after we receive your content, requirements and initial deposit.

Do I own the website after payment?

Yes. After full payment, you receive ownership of the approved website files and content, excluding third-party licensed software, premium themes, plugins and paid services, which remain subject to their own licences.

What if I need more pages or features than my package includes?

That's no problem — additional pages or features can be added as a scoped add-on and quoted separately, or you can upgrade to the next package before development begins.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

Yes, as add-ons. Domain pricing depends on the extension you choose, and shared hosting starts from 10 KWD/year depending on storage and traffic needs. Business-grade hosting for higher-traffic or e-commerce sites is quoted separately.

What is the payment schedule?

A 50% deposit is required to begin the project, with the remaining 50% due before final launch. Larger Business Pro and Enterprise projects may use milestone-based payments instead.

Can I request revisions after launch?

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds during development. Changes requested after launch are handled as ongoing website maintenance, available from 15 KWD/month or on a per-request basis.
