Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

KhurshedTech IT Solutions ("KhurshedTech", "we", "us") provides managed IT services to businesses in Kuwait and remotely worldwide. This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information This Website Collects

This site does not run its own server-side database. When you use the contact form or the quote calculator's lead form, the details you enter (name, company, email, phone, service needed, budget, and message) are used only to prepare a pre-filled WhatsApp message, which opens in your own WhatsApp app or web client. We do not store form submissions on this website.

Analytics & Cookies

This site may use Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, and Google Search Console to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, button clicks, approximate location and device type). These tools may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser. You can control or block these via your browser's privacy settings.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries you send us via WhatsApp, email, or the contact form.
  • To prepare quotations and proposals for services you've requested.
  • To improve this website based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Client Data During Service Delivery

When you engage KhurshedTech for IT support, we may have access to your business systems, accounts, or data as required to deliver the service. Any such access is governed by the agreement made directly with you, and can be covered by a signed NDA on request. We do not publish client names, internal IP addresses, screenshots, or confidential business information without your explicit permission.

Third-Party Links

This site links to WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram and Google Maps. Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing the information you share with them directly.

This policy is provided for general transparency and does not constitute legal advice. If your business requires a policy tailored to specific regulatory obligations (e.g. GDPR, PDPL), please consult a qualified lawyer.
